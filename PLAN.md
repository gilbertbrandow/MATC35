The weekly programme is preliminary and may be updated. Section references and exercise numbers are to David M. Burton, Elementary Number Theory, 7th edition.

The compulsory written assignment is a handwritten weekly course journal. Each published entry is due before the Friday lecture in the following week, and all but one of the assigned entries must be submitted to pass the course.
