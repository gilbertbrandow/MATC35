The weekly programme is preliminary and may be updated. Section references and exercise numbers are to David M. Burton, Elementary Number Theory, 7th edition.

The compulsory written assignment is a handwritten weekly course journal. Each journal topic covers the previous lecture week, and the entry is due before the Friday lecture in the following week. All but one of the assigned entries must be submitted to pass the course.

Canvas numbers its exercise weeks by seminar (Week 1 = seminar 4 November, Week 2 = seminar 11 November, and so on). Each seminar discusses the material from the previous lecture week.
